# Story — Aarav Mehta (FICTIONAL)

> Everything below is invented for teaching. Any resemblance to a real person is
> coincidental. Do not use real data.

**Aarav Mehta**, 24, fictional resident of "Nimbua City".
Public footprint (all fictional):
- Handle `@aarav_rides` used on three platforms.
- Public post: "Finally joined **NimbuaTech** as a junior analyst! #firstjob"
- Photo at "Café Verde, MG Road" tagged with location on.
- Comment: "Taking Bruno (my labrador) to the vet tomorrow 9am."
- Bio: "Nimbua • cricket • coffee • NimbuaTech"

**What an attacker assembles:**
Name, age bracket, employer, neighbourhood, daily routine, pet's name, interests.

**The convincing call (fictional):**
"Hi Aarav, this is HR at NimbuaTech. Quick payroll verification — and how's Bruno
after the vet?" — Instantly trusted, because the facts check out.

**Teaching point:** The scammer didn't hack anything. Aarav published his own
profile, one harmless post at a time.
