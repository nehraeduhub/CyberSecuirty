## 2. The HOOK formula (first 7 seconds of EVERY video)
Open on your face or a full-screen scam message — **never** a channel intro.
Pattern: **Shock fact / question → promise → “rukiye, main dikhata hoon.”**
> Example: *“Ek phone call aur 80 saal ke uncle ke 42 lakh gaye. ‘Digital arrest’ —
> yeh scam India mein sabse zyada paisa loot raha hai. Aaj main dikhata hoon yeh kaise
> hota hai, aur aap kaise 10 second mein pakad sakte ho.”*

Rules: say the number, name the victim-type, promise the save, then deliver. Keep
the channel logo/outro for the END. First 30 seconds decide watch-time.

## 3. The repeatable episode structure (use the portal deck live)
1. **Hook (0:00–0:20)** — shock fact + promise (above).
2. **The reality (0:20–1:30)** — India data slide (real ₹ numbers). Builds urgency.
3. **The story / what the victim sees (1:30–3:30)** — screen-share the deck’s mockup
   (fake call / SMS / UPI screen). “Imagine yeh message aapke phone pe aaya…”
4. **How the attacker does it (3:30–5:30)** — the animated flow diagram. Reveal the trick.
5. **Red flags (5:30–7:00)** — the deck’s red-flags slide. “In 4 signs ko yaad rakho.”
6. **Protection (7:00–9:00)** — the protect slide + the one-line rule.
7. **Optional Kali practical (where tagged)** — show the tool on your laptop (fictional data).
8. **CTA + outro (last 30s)** — “Call 1930 · report cybercrime.gov.in · share with family · subscribe.”

> Tip: open the deck in **Script: OFF** so viewers see clean visuals; glance at your
> second screen for the Hinglish script.

## 7. Shorts / Reels strategy (2–3 per episode)
Cut vertical 30–50s clips from each episode:
- **Clip A — the hook + one red flag** (highest reach).
- **Clip B — the mockup reveal** (“real or fake?” → answer).
- **Clip C — the one-line rule** (“PIN = PAY”, “Police don’t arrest on video call”).
Add big captions (watched on mute), end with “Full video + free course in bio.”
Post Shorts on off-days to keep the algorithm warm between long videos.

## 8. Titles &amp; thumbnails — rules that work
- Numbers + a victim + a save: *“₹42 lakh gone — here’s the 10-second catch.”*
- Curiosity gap, not lies: never promise hacking or “get your money back tricks.”
- Thumbnail: 3–4 BIG words, one face/emotion, one scam object (SMS/call/UPI), high contrast.
- Keep brand colour (navy + orange/green) consistent so the series is recognisable.

## 9. Safety &amp; platform-policy guardrails (important)
- **Teach defence, never attack.** Say it on camera. No step-by-step to victimise others.
- Use **fictional data / your own accounts** in every demo (your portal already does).
- Kali tools: only on fictional/authorised data, labelled “authorised demonstration only.”
- Sensitive topics (sextortion): focus on support + reporting; no explicit content.
- Add the 1930 / cybercrime.gov.in helpline in every video &amp; description (platforms favour this).
- Don’t show real victims’ data, real OTPs, or real bank screens.

## 10. Recording setup (keep it simple)
- Screen-share the portal (deck in Script:OFF) + a webcam corner (picture-in-picture).
- Second monitor/phone showing the Hinglish script (Script:ON there).
- Mic close, quiet room, 1080p, 30fps. OBS Studio (free) to record + go live.
- Batch-record 2–3 episodes in one sitting; publish weekly.
