# Digital Arrest — Red Flags & Correct Response

## Red flags (any ONE means scam)
1. The phrase **"digital arrest"** — it is not a real thing.
2. A parcel/Aadhaar "misuse" case you know nothing about.
3. Police/CBI/Customs/RBI **on a video call**.
4. "Stay on camera, don't disconnect, tell no one."
5. "Transfer money to a verification/RBI account; it's refundable."
6. Threats of immediate, non-bailable arrest to stop you thinking.
7. Pressure to act in minutes; refusal to let you verify or call back.

## What the victim SHOULD do
- **Disconnect.** You are never legally obliged to stay on a call.
- **Do not transfer any money.** No agency works this way.
- **Talk to someone** — family, a friend, a colleague. Isolation is the weapon.
- **Verify independently:** call the real police on **112**, cyber helpline **1930**.
- **Report:** **cybercrime.gov.in** and your bank immediately if money moved.

## How to prevent it (teach the family)
- Agree now: "No real police arrests you over a video call. Ever."
- Seniors especially: if a caller claims to be police/CBI/courier about a
  "case", **hang up and call a family member**.
- Banks/RBI never ask you to move money to "verify" it.
- Keep calm; scammers rely on panic. Buying 10 minutes defeats the scam.

## One-line takeaway
> "Police don't arrest you on WhatsApp. Hang up, breathe, and call 1930."
