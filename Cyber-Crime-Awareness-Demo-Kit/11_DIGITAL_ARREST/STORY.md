# 11 · Digital Arrest — Story
**Tech Guardians · AUTHORISED DEMONSTRATION ONLY**

**Objective:** Expose the "digital arrest" scam — fake police/CBI who trap a
victim on a video call, manufacture fear, and extort money. One of India's most
damaging scams, especially for seniors and professionals.

**Time Required:** 10–12 minutes (role-play + debrief)
**Difficulty:** Beginner to present
**Internet:** Not required.

## The scam in one line
> There is **no such thing** as a "digital arrest". Indian police/CBI/courts
> NEVER arrest, interrogate or take payment over a video call.

## The story (fictional victim: "Mr. Sharma", 58)
Mr. Sharma gets a call: a "courier company" says a parcel in his name — sent to
Taiwan/China — contains illegal items (drugs/fake passports). The call is
"transferred" to "Mumbai Police", then "CBI". A uniformed "officer" appears on a
video call with a police-looking backdrop. They show a fake "arrest warrant" and
"RBI notice" with Sharma's name. They say he's under **"digital arrest"**: he must
stay on camera, tell no one, and move his money to a "RBI verification account"
to prove it's clean. Terrified, isolated, and on camera for hours, he transfers
his savings. The money is gone.

## Why it works
Authority (police/CBI) + fear (criminal case, jail) + isolation ("tell no one")
+ urgency ("transfer now or be arrested") + realistic props (uniforms, fake
documents, spoofed numbers).
