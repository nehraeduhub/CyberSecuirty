# Digital Arrest — Role-Play Script (FICTIONAL)
Two presenters: ATTACKER (A) and VICTIM (V). A third voice, PRESENTER (P),
pauses to explain. Keep it slightly theatrical — the fear is the lesson.

---
**A (as 'FedEx courier'):** "Sir, this is regarding a parcel booked in your name
and Aadhaar to Taiwan. It contains 4 passports and 150g MDMA. The parcel is
seized by Mumbai Customs."
**V:** "What? I never sent any parcel!"
**A:** "Sir, your Aadhaar is misused then. I am transferring you to the Cyber
Crime Branch. Do not disconnect — it is a legal line."

> **P (pause):** Notice the opener — a crime in YOUR name, instant panic, and
> "don't disconnect". The hook is set in 20 seconds.

---
**A (as 'Inspector', video on, uniform, 'police' backdrop):** "I am Inspector
from Mumbai Cyber Branch. A case is registered against you under money laundering.
You are now under **digital arrest**. Keep your camera ON at all times. You may
not leave the room or contact anyone."
**V:** "Digital arrest? I don't understand…"
**A:** "It means you are in custody online. Disconnecting is obstruction — a
non-bailable offence. Do you want to go to jail?"

> **P (pause):** There is NO such law. "Digital arrest" does not exist. Real
> police never keep you on a video call or forbid you from contacting people.

---
**A (as 'CBI officer'):** "To prove your money is not from crime, transfer it to
this RBI verification account. It will be returned in 2 hours after audit. This
is confidential — tell NO ONE, not even family, or they become co-accused."
**V:** "I'm scared… how much?"
**A:** "All liquid funds. Act now. The warrant is ready. Officers are outside."

> **P (pause):** The goal was always the money. 'RBI verification account',
> 'refundable', 'tell no one' — RBI has no such account, and no agency asks you
> to transfer money to prove innocence.

---
**V transfers money. Screen freezes.**
**P:** "By the time Mr. Sharma told his son, the money and the 'officers' had
vanished. Let's break down exactly how he could have stopped this at every step."
