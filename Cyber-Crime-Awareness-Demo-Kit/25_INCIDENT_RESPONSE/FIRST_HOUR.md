# First Hour & Beyond
**Tech Guardians · AWARENESS ONLY**

- **File a report at cybercrime.gov.in** with all evidence (keep the ack number).
- **Written complaint to the bank** within the timelines (ask about zero/limited
  liability for unauthorised transactions; report fast to qualify).
- **Secure your identity:** if ID/Aadhaar/PAN was shared, monitor for misuse;
  lock Aadhaar biometrics (UIDAI); watch your credit report.
- **Phone/SIM:** if SIM-swap suspected, contact your telecom; check
  sancharsaathi.gov.in for SIMs issued in your name.
- **Devices:** if a malicious app was installed, disconnect internet, uninstall or
  factory-reset, change all passwords afterward from a clean device.
- **Social accounts:** if taken over, use official recovery; warn contacts.
- **Emotional support:** scams exploit emotion; it's not your fault — seek support,
  especially for sextortion cases (do not pay, preserve evidence, report).
