# If You're Scammed — First 15 Minutes
**Tech Guardians · AWARENESS ONLY**

Speed matters most for financial fraud — fast action can freeze the money.

1. **Call 1930** (Cyber Crime Helpline) immediately — the sooner, the better the
   chance of freezing the transfer.
2. **Call your bank/UPI app** on the official number; ask to block the card/
   account and report the fraudulent transaction.
3. **Stop further loss:** disconnect the call, don't send more money, don't
   install anything they asked for.
4. **Don't delete anything:** keep messages, numbers, screenshots, transaction IDs.
5. **Change passwords** of the affected account from a safe device; enable MFA.
6. **Tell someone** you trust — you'll think more clearly and avoid shame-driven
   secrecy the scammer relied on.
