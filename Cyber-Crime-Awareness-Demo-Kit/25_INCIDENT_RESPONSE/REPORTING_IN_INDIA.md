# Reporting Cyber Crime in India
**Tech Guardians · AWARENESS ONLY**

## Fastest help
- **Call 1930** — National Cyber Crime Helpline (financial fraud; money-freeze window).
- **112** — Police emergency.

## Online
- **cybercrime.gov.in** — National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal.
  - General complaints, and a dedicated section for **Women/Child** related crimes
    (can be filed anonymously).
  - Keep the **acknowledgement number**.

## Other official resources
- **Your bank/UPI provider** — report unauthorised transactions immediately.
- **sancharsaathi.gov.in** (DoT) — lost phone, SIMs in your name, report fraud numbers.
- **CERT-In (cert-in.org.in)** — for organisations/incident reporting.
- **UIDAI** — lock/unlock Aadhaar biometrics.

## Tips
- Report **fast** — timelines matter for both money recovery and bank liability.
- Give complete evidence (see EVIDENCE_COLLECTION.md).
- Beware "recovery agents" who promise to get your money back for a fee — that's a
  second scam. Use only official channels.
