# Safety & Authorisation Rules (READ FIRST)

This kit exists to **protect people**, not to attack anyone. These rules are not
optional.

## The 10 rules
1. **Fictional / dummy / authorised data only.** Use fictional identities,
   dummy credentials, your own devices/accounts, public test data, or systems
   you have **written authorisation** to test. Nothing else.
2. **Never target real people's accounts** — no WhatsApp, Instagram, banking,
   email or Wi-Fi that isn't yours or explicitly authorised.
3. **Never capture real secrets.** No real credentials, OTPs, cookies, tokens,
   card numbers or Aadhaar. The phishing demo is local and stores nothing.
4. **No malware.** This kit builds no spyware, ransomware, stealers or malicious
   APKs. APK work here is read-only *analysis* of samples you are allowed to use.
5. **No SMS/OTP bombing, no DoS, no mass targeting.** Ever.
6. **Label offensive tools.** Any offensive-security tool is used only in an
   "AUTHORISED DEMONSTRATION ONLY" context.
7. **Get consent to show faces/data.** If a volunteer participates, use dummy
   accounts you created for the demo — never their real personal accounts.
8. **Respect privacy laws** (incl. the Indian IT Act and the DPDP Act). When in
   doubt, use the sample data in `../24_SAMPLE_DATA/`.
9. **Online lookups: self or fiction only.** Run breach/OSINT lookups on your
   own email/number or on the fictional samples provided — never on an audience
   member without their explicit consent.
10. **Purpose test.** Before any demo ask: *"Does this teach people to protect
    themselves?"* If the honest answer is "no, it teaches attacking," don't do it.

## Authorisation checklist (keep a signed copy for institutional events)
- [ ] Organiser has approved the content and demos.
- [ ] All demo accounts/devices are mine or dummy.
- [ ] No live service belonging to an audience member will be touched.
- [ ] Sample data only; no real personal data collected.
- [ ] Offline fallback ready if Wi-Fi/online tools fail.

## If an audience member brings a real case
Do **not** investigate their real accounts on stage. Point them to
`../25_INCIDENT_RESPONSE/` and the India reporting steps (helpline **1930**,
**cybercrime.gov.in**). Help, don't demonstrate on their live data.
