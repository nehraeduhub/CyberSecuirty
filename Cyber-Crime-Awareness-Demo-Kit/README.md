# Cyber Crime Awareness Live Demo Kit

**Think Like a Hacker — Protect Like a Defender.**

A complete, ready-to-run awareness toolkit for cyber-security seminars in
schools, colleges, training institutes and Indian government organisations.

> **Brought to you by Tech Guardians**
> Mentor: **Rj Nehra — Master in Cyber Security**

---

## What this is (and is NOT)

This kit teaches ordinary people **how they get targeted** by cyber criminals and
**how to protect themselves**. Every demonstration is defensive, legal and
awareness-focused.

| This kit IS | This kit is NOT |
|---|---|
| Awareness demonstrations using fictional data | A hacking tutorial |
| OSINT shown on **your own** / dummy / authorised data | A way to attack real people |
| A **local** phishing page that never stores anything | A credential stealer |
| Mock scam screens and role-plays | Real OTP/SMS bombing |
| Teaching victims the red flags | Malware / spyware / APK builders |

**Golden rule:** Only legal, ethical, authorised demonstrations. Fictional
identities, dummy credentials, your own devices/accounts, public test data, or
explicitly authorised systems — nothing else.

## Safety & authorisation policy

Read `00_START_HERE/SAFETY_RULES.md` before any live session. Short version:

1. Demonstrate only on fictional data, dummy accounts, your own devices, or
   systems you have **written authorisation** to test.
2. Never capture real credentials, OTPs, cookies or tokens.
3. Any offensive-security tool is labelled **"AUTHORISED DEMONSTRATION ONLY"**.
4. The goal is always protection, never attack.
