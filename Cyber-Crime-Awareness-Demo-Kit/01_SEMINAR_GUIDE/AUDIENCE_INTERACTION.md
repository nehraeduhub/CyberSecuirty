# Audience Interaction Playbook

Engagement makes awareness stick. Use these throughout.

## Show-of-hands openers
- "Who has received a message saying they won a prize?"
- "Who has a password they use on more than one website?" *(almost everyone)*
- "Who has scanned a QR code without reading what it said?"

## Live "spot the scam" (works in any audience)
Show two messages/screens from `../24_SAMPLE_DATA/`. Ask: *"Real or fake? Shout
it out."* Then reveal the red flags.

## Volunteer demo (safe)
Ask for a volunteer. Use a **dummy account you created**, never their real one.
Let them "fall for" the local phishing page — then reveal it was simulated.
Big laugh, big lesson, zero harm.

## Age-specific framing
- **School:** gaming accounts, free-diamonds scams, fake giveaways, deepfakes.
- **College:** fake job/internship, investment/trading apps, loan apps.
- **Govt staff:** spear-phishing, fake KYC, official-looking notices, data leaks.
- **Seniors:** digital arrest, fake bank/electricity calls, UPI "refund".

## Questions to keep asking
- "What emotion is this message trying to create?"
- "How would you verify this independently?"
- "Who would you call to check?"

## Close the loop
End interactions with the protection step — never leave the room scared without
a clear action.
