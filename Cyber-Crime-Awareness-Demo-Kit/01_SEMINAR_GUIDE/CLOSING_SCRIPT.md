# Closing Script

"Let's bring it together. Today you saw how a criminal thinks — they use
**authority, urgency, fear and greed**, and a little public information about you.
None of it is magic. It's a trick, and now you can see the trick coming.

Remember three things:
1. **Slow down.** Every scam needs you to act fast. Real institutions let you
   wait, verify and call back on an official number.
2. **No one legitimate needs your OTP, PIN or full card number.** No one. Ever.
3. **If something feels wrong, it is.** Hang up. Verify. Ask someone.

If you are ever targeted, you are not alone and it's not your fault. In India,
call **1930** and report at **cybercrime.gov.in** — fast reporting can freeze the
money.

Take the handout, share it with your parents and grandparents — they're the most
targeted. You are now a **Guardian** for your family.

Think like a hacker. Protect like a defender. Thank you."
