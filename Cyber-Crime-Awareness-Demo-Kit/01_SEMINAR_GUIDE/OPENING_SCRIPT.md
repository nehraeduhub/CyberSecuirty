# Opening Script

> *(Walk in calm. Don't mention tools yet.)*

"Good morning. Before we begin — a quick question. **Raise your hand if you have
a smartphone.**" *(Most hands go up.)*

"Keep it up if you use **UPI, WhatsApp, or online banking**." *(Hands stay up.)*

"Thank you. Every hand you see is also something a cyber criminal sees — an
opportunity. But here's the good news: the same thinking criminals use to
target you is the thinking that protects you. That's our theme today —"

> **"Think Like a Hacker — Protect Like a Defender."**

"I'm from **Tech Guardians**. I won't teach you to attack anyone. Everything I
show today uses **fake identities and my own test accounts** — it's an
**authorised demonstration only**. My goal is simple: when the real scam comes to
your phone — and it will — you'll recognise it in two seconds.

Let me start not with technology, but with a message. **Imagine your phone rings
right now, and the caller says he's from the police…**"

> *(Transition into the first demo — usually Digital Arrest or a scam story.)*
