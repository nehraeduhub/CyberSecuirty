# UPI Safety Rules (handout-ready)

1. **PIN = PAY.** Entering your UPI PIN always means money LEAVES your account.
2. **Receiving money needs nothing from you** — no PIN, no OTP, no approval.
3. **Never approve a "request money"/collect** you didn't initiate.
4. **No refund needs your PIN or OTP.** That's always a scam.
5. **Never install remote/screen-share apps** on a support call.
6. **Get helpline numbers from the official app or card**, never from Google ads.
7. **Verify credits in your own bank app**, not via someone's screenshot.
8. **Set a daily UPI limit** you're comfortable with.
9. **If scammed:** call your bank + **1930** immediately, then cybercrime.gov.in.
10. **Slow down.** Urgency is the scam.
