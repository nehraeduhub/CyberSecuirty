# 12 · UPI Fraud — "Refund, collect request & fake support"
**Tech Guardians · AUTHORISED DEMONSTRATION ONLY**

**Objective:** Show the common UPI tricks — fake refund, collect/"request money",
QR-to-pay, and fake customer care — and the one rule that defeats them all.

**Time Required:** 7–9 minutes
**Difficulty:** Beginner
**Internet:** Not required. **No real transaction is performed.**

## What You Need
- Mock screens in `MOCK_PAYMENT_SCREENS/`; `SAFETY_RULES.md`.

## The one rule
> **Your UPI PIN only ever lets money LEAVE your account.**
> Nobody needs your PIN/OTP to SEND you money or to give a refund.

## Step-by-Step
1. **Fake refund:** "We over-charged you ₹2,000, accept this request to get it."
   → it's a **collect request**; approving it PAYS them.
2. **Collect request:** show how "₹1 request" conditions people to approve blindly.
3. **QR-to-pay:** (link to demo 09) scanning to "receive" actually pays.
4. **Fake customer care:** a Googled "support number" that asks you to install a
   screen-share app / enter PIN.

## What Audience Sees
Four different wrappers around the same move: trick you into **authorising a
debit** or revealing your PIN/OTP.

## Presenter Script
"You call what you think is your bank's helpline — actually a number a scammer
planted online. 'Sir, for the refund, approve the request we sent and enter your
PIN.' The moment you do, your money is gone. A refund never needs your PIN."

## Security Lesson
UPI is safe; the human step is targeted. PIN/OTP + "approve this request" +
screen-sharing apps are the three danger signals.

## Prevention
- Never enter your UPI PIN to **receive** money or a **refund**.
- Never approve a "collect/request money" you didn't initiate.
- Never install AnyDesk/TeamViewer/"QuickSupport" on a support call.
- Find helpline numbers only on the **official app / card back**, never from search ads.
- Check credits in your **own bank app**, not the other party's "screenshot".

## Questions to Ask
- "When money COMES to you, does UPI ask your PIN?" (No.)
- "Who has searched a 'customer care number' on Google?"

## Safety Warning
Mock screens only. Never perform a real UPI transaction during the demo.
