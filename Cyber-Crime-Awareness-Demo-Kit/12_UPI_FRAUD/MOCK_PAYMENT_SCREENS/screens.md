# Mock UPI screens (FICTIONAL — no real payment)

## A — Fake refund via collect request
```
[ NimbuPay ]  Payment request
 From:  "SHOPDESK REFUND"
 Amount: ₹2,000.00
 Note:  "Accept to receive your refund"
 [ DECLINE ]              [ PAY ₹2,000 ]   <-- "accept" = you PAY ₹2,000
 Enter UPI PIN to proceed  ■■■■■■
```
Reality: approving a *collect request* DEBITS you. A refund is a credit; it never
needs your action or PIN.

## B — Fake customer care
```
Google: "nimbupay customer care number"
 → Ad/result: +91 9XXXXXXXXX  (planted by scammer)
 Agent: "Install 'QuickSupport' so I can process your refund."
 Agent: "Now open your UPI app and enter your PIN to sync..."
```
Reality: real support never needs screen-share + your PIN.

## C — QR "scan to receive"
See demo 09. Scanning a received QR + entering PIN = you paid.

## The giveaways
- You must enter a **PIN** to "receive".
- A **collect request** you didn't start.
- A **support number** from search/ads, not the official app.
- A request to **install a screen-sharing app**.
