# Mock WhatsApp chats (FICTIONAL)

## 1 — The takeover bait (attacker → you, posing as a friend/support)
> "⚠️ WhatsApp Security: unusual login detected. To keep your account, reply with
>  the 6-digit code we just sent to your number."
> "Please hurry, your account will be locked in 5 minutes."

## 2 — The 'friend' variant
> "Hey sorry!! I entered your number by mistake for my new phone. You'll get a
>  code — can you send it to me? 🙏 I'll fix it."

## 3 — After takeover (attacker → victim's contacts, AS the victim)
> "Hey, are you free? I'm in an emergency and my UPI isn't working."
> "Can you send ₹8,000 to this number? I'll return it tonight, promise 🙏"
> "Please don't call, I'm in a meeting, just text."  ← *refuses a call = red flag*

## Red flags
- Asks for a code/OTP "sent to you".
- Urgency and a countdown.
- Avoids a voice/video call.
- Money request from a "friend" to a new/unknown number.
