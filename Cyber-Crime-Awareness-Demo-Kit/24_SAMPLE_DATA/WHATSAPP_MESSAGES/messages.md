# FICTIONAL WhatsApp scam messages

## Code request (takeover)
"Hi! I accidentally sent my WhatsApp verification code to your number. Can you
forward it to me? Thanks 🙏"

## Family emergency (voice/text)
"Beta, this is uncle's new number. I'm stuck and my UPI failed. Send ₹6,000 now,
I'll return by evening. Don't call, I'm in a meeting."

## Investment group
"🚀 Today's stock tip gave 32% in 2 hours! Join VIP, deposit to start. Limited seats!"

## Job/task
"Earn ₹3,000/day liking videos from home. Small refundable deposit to activate
your account. Reply START."

## Red flags
Code/OTP request, refuses a call, urgency, pay-to-earn, too-good returns.
