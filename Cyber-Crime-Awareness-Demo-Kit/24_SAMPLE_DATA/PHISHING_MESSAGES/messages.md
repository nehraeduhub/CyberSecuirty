# FICTIONAL phishing messages (email/SMS)

## Bank blocked
"Dear Customer, your account is temporarily suspended due to incomplete KYC.
Verify within 24h to avoid permanent block: http://bank.account-verify.example/kyc"

## Income tax refund
"You have a pending refund of ₹14,500. Confirm bank details to receive:
http://incometax-refund.example.co/claim"  (wrong TLD; not .gov.in)

## Prize
"Congratulations! Your number won ₹10,00,000 in the KBC lucky draw. Share your
details and a ₹2,000 processing fee to claim."

## Delivery
"Your parcel is on hold due to unpaid customs ₹35. Pay now:
https://exmpl.link/x9Fk2"  (shortened link hides destination)

## Red flags across all
Urgency, lookalike/short links, fees to 'receive', requests for OTP/details.
