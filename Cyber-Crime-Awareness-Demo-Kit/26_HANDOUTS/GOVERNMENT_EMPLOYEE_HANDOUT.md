# Government Employee Handout
**Tech Guardians · Think Like a Hacker — Protect Like a Defender**

## Threats to official work
- Spear-phishing & fake internal portals / KYC pages.
- Malicious attachments/APKs; "urgent" emails impersonating seniors.
- Data leaks from oversharing and weak passwords.
- Social engineering calls seeking information or access.

## Office cyber hygiene
1. Verify unusual requests (fund transfer, data, access) via a second channel.
2. Never reuse official passwords; use MFA; lock your screen.
3. Don't install unapproved software/APKs; report suspicious mail to IT/CERT-In.
4. Handle citizen/official data on least-privilege basis; don't forward to personal email.
5. Beware public Wi-Fi for official work; use approved VPN.
6. Strip metadata from documents before external sharing.
7. Report incidents promptly (internal IT + CERT-In); preserve evidence.

## Personal protection carries over
- 1930 / cybercrime.gov.in for personal fraud.
- Watch for digital-arrest, UPI, KYC and courier scams targeting staff & families.
