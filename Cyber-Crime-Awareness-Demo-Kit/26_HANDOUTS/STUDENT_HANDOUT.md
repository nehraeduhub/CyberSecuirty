# Student Handout
**Tech Guardians · Think Like a Hacker — Protect Like a Defender**

## Scams aimed at you
- Free game currency / "verify your gaming account" phishing.
- Fake giveaways & influencer "double your money".
- Deepfake/AI-voice pranks and blackmail.
- Fake internships/jobs asking for a deposit.
- Social media account takeover & impersonation.

## Your 6 habits
1. Never share OTP/password — not even with "a friend" asking for a code.
2. Don't click links in DMs/SMS; go to the official app.
3. Use a strong passphrase + 2FA on email, socials and gaming.
4. Lock down privacy; don't post live location or ID documents.
5. Be careful with strangers online, video calls and "go private" requests.
6. Tell a trusted adult if something feels wrong — fast. It's not your fault.

## Remember
- Report: **1930** / **cybercrime.gov.in** (and women/child section exists).
- Be a **Guardian** — teach your parents and grandparents these rules.
