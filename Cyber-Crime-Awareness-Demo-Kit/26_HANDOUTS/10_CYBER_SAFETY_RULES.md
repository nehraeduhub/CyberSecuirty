# 10 Cyber Safety Rules (Print & Share)
**Tech Guardians · Rj Nehra — Master in Cyber Security**

1. **Slow down.** Every scam needs you to rush. Pause and verify.
2. **Never share OTP/PIN/CVV/password.** No bank or officer ever needs them.
3. **PIN = PAY.** You never enter a UPI PIN to *receive* money.
4. **Don't log in from links.** Open the official app or type the address yourself.
5. **No "digital arrest" exists.** Police/CBI never arrest or take money on a video call.
6. **Install apps only from official stores.** Check permissions.
7. **Use strong, unique passphrases + MFA.** Let a password manager remember them.
8. **Verify callers independently.** Hang up and call the official number.
9. **Think before you post.** Location, routine and photos reveal a lot.
10. **If hit, act fast:** call **1930**, your bank, and report at **cybercrime.gov.in**.

> Think Like a Hacker — Protect Like a Defender.
