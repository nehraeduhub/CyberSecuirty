# Senior Citizen Handout (Large, Simple)
**Tech Guardians · Stay Safe Online**

## The 5 calls/messages to distrust
1. "You are under **digital arrest**." → NOT REAL. Hang up. Call family.
2. "Your bank KYC/account is blocked, verify now." → Go to your branch instead.
3. "Scan this QR to **receive** money." → You never scan/enter PIN to receive.
4. "Electricity will be cut tonight, pay now." → Check the official bill only.
5. "I'm your relative, in trouble, send money, don't tell anyone." → Call them back.

## Simple rules
- **Never share OTP, PIN, or password** with anyone on a call.
- **Never pay to 'receive' money or a refund.**
- **Don't install any app** someone on a call tells you to.
- **Slow down.** Real officials let you check and call back.
- **Ask family** before sending money or sharing details.

## If something happens
- Call **1930** and your **bank** right away.
- Tell a family member — you are not alone, and it is not your fault.

> Keep this near your phone. When in doubt — stop and call family.
