# 07 · Location — "You told them where you are"
**Tech Guardians · AUTHORISED DEMONSTRATION ONLY**

**Objective:** Show how routine social posts reveal real-time location, routine
and absence (great for burglars, stalkers and scammers).

**Time Required:** 5–6 minutes
**Difficulty:** Beginner
**Internet:** Not required (use the sample posts). 100% offline-capable.

## What You Need
- The sample posts in `SAMPLE_SOCIAL_POSTS/`.

## Step-by-Step
1. Read the sample posts aloud one by one.
2. After each, ask: *"What did that just reveal?"*
3. Build a timeline: home → office → gym → "on vacation!" (house empty).
4. Show how a scammer uses "I know you're travelling" to pressure family.

## What Audience Sees
A week of harmless posts reconstructed into a predictable, exploitable routine.

## Presenter Script
"'Airport! Goa for 5 days 🎉' means your house is empty for 5 days — you just
told the whole internet. Later, a caller tells your parent 'your son is stranded
in Goa, send money.' The post made the lie believable."

## Security Lesson
Location + routine + absence are the three gifts over-sharing gives attackers.

## Prevention
- Post **after** you leave, not during ("throwback" not "live").
- Turn off live location sharing except with trusted contacts.
- Keep travel plans off public feeds.
- Agree a family "safe word" to defeat 'relative in trouble' scams.

## Questions to Ask
- "Who posts while they're still at the location?"
- "Would your last 5 posts let me guess where you are tomorrow at 6pm?"

## Safety Warning
Use only the provided sample posts. Do not analyse an audience member's real feed.
