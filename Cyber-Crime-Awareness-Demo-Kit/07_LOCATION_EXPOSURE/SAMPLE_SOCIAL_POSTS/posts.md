1. "Morning run done! Same park, same 6am 💪 #routine"  → *home area + schedule*
2. "Coffee at Café Verde before office ☕"               → *route + workplace*
3. "New ID badge finally! @NimbuaTech"  *(photo of badge)* → *employer + badge look*
4. "Family dinner at home tonight, all 5 of us ❤️"       → *household size*
5. "AIRPORT!! Goa for 5 days 🎉🏖️ #vacation"             → *house empty 5 days*
6. "Hotel sea-view 😍 room 412"                           → *exact location abroad*
7. "Back home, exhausted but happy!"                       → *return time confirmed*

## Timeline an attacker builds
- Knows your morning spot (6am park) and workplace.
- Knows what your office badge looks like (impersonation).
- Knows your house is empty for 5 days (burglary / 'relative stranded' scam).
- Knows exactly when you return.

**All from public posts — no hacking.**
