# Shodan — Safety & Ethics Guidelines

## Allowed (awareness)
- Viewing aggregate statistics ("how many devices expose X").
- Looking up **your own** public IP / assets.
- Looking up systems you have **written authorisation** to assess.

## NOT allowed (and often illegal)
- Logging into, probing, or "just checking" a device that isn't yours.
- Using default credentials against any third-party system.
- Collecting or sharing data from exposed systems.

## Presenter rules
1. Never open/connect to a stranger's device on screen.
2. Keep searches at the category/statistics level for the crowd.
3. Emphasise the DEFENSE: close exposure, change defaults, update, VPN.
4. If the audience is technical, point them to responsible disclosure, not access.

## The defender takeaway
> "Being findable isn't the crime — being exposed is the risk. Shut the door:
> defaults, updates, firewall, VPN."
