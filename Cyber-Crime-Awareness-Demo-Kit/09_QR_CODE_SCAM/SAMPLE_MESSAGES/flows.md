# Mock UPI QR flows (FICTIONAL — no real payment)

## ✅ Genuine "RECEIVE" (what really happens)
1. You tell the buyer your UPI ID or show YOUR QR.
2. Buyer enters amount in THEIR app and authorises with THEIR PIN.
3. Your app shows: "₹5,000 CREDITED". **You entered no PIN.**

## ❌ Scam "scan to receive" (the trap)
1. "Seller" gets a QR/link from the "buyer": *"Scan to receive ₹5,000."*
2. Seller scans → app shows a **COLLECT / PAY ₹5,000** request.
3. App asks for the seller's **UPI PIN**.
4. Seller enters PIN → **₹5,000 is DEBITED from the seller.**

## The one-line rule
> **PIN = PAY.** You never enter a UPI PIN to receive money.

## Sample scam chat (read aloud)
> Buyer: "Interested in your phone! Sending ₹5,000 advance now."
> Buyer: "Got an error — can you scan this QR so the money comes to you?"
> Buyer: "Hurry, the amount is on hold, it'll expire in 5 minutes!"
*(Urgency + 'scan to receive' + 'on hold' = classic UPI QR scam.)*
