# Gophish (Authorised Awareness Campaigns)
**TOOL PURPOSE:** Run CONTROLLED, INTERNAL phishing-SIMULATION campaigns to measure
and train staff awareness — with management authorisation.

**INSTALLATION:** Download the official release from getgophish.com and run the
`gophish` binary; admin UI opens on `https://127.0.0.1:3333`.

**OPEN/RUN:** Launch `./gophish`, log in locally, create a campaign aimed ONLY at
your own organisation's consenting staff/test inboxes.

**WHAT TO SHOW:** The dashboard concept: who opened/clicked a SIMULATED training
email, followed by a teaching landing page.

**WHAT THE AUDIENCE LEARNS:** Organisations test themselves to find and coach
at-risk users — simulation, not entrapment.

**SAFETY WARNING:** ONLY with written management authorisation, ONLY against your
own org's consenting users/test accounts, and the landing page must be a TRAINING
page that captures no real credentials. Never target outsiders or real brands.
AUTHORISED DEMONSTRATION ONLY.

**DEMO DATA:** your own test inboxes; the local awareness page (demo 08) as the
landing page.

**TROUBLESHOOTING:** For a seminar, prefer SHOWING screenshots of a past
authorised campaign rather than sending live mail.
