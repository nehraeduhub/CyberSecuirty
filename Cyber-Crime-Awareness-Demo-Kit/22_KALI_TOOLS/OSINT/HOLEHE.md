# Holehe (Email → Account Discovery)
**TOOL PURPOSE:** Check which websites an email address is registered on (without logging in)
— to show how one email maps your online footprint.

**INSTALLATION:**
```bash
pipx install holehe
```

**OPEN/RUN:** `holehe you@example.com`  (YOUR OWN email, or a reserved sample)

**WHAT TO SHOW:** The list of services where the email is registered.

**WHAT THE AUDIENCE LEARNS:** One reused email ties your accounts together; attackers use this
to target password resets and phishing (ties to demo 03).

**SAFETY WARNING:** YOUR OWN / fictional email only. Never profile someone else. AUTHORISED
DEMONSTRATION ONLY.

**DEMO DATA:** `../../03_EMAIL_BREACH/SAMPLE_DATA/sample_emails.txt`.

**TROUBLESHOOTING:** Rate limits/false negatives are normal; present the concept, not completeness.
