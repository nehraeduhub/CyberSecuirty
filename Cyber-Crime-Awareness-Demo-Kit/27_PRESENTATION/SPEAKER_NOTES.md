# Speaker Notes
**Tech Guardians · Think Like a Hacker — Protect Like a Defender**

- **Open with story, not tools.** (See `../01_SEMINAR_GUIDE/OPENING_SCRIPT.md`.)
- **State the ethics once, early:** "Everything is a simulation on my own/fake
  data — an authorised demonstration only."
- **Per demo:** hook → what victim sees → why it works → reveal tech → red flags →
  protect → one-line takeaway (the 7 beats in `../00_START_HERE/DEMO_FLOW.md`).
- **Pace:** 5–8 min per demo; pause after each "reveal".
- **Engage:** show-of-hands and "real or fake?" (`AUDIENCE_INTERACTION.md`).
- **Tone:** empowering, not scary. Always end on protection.
- **Localise:** pick scams matching the audience (students/staff/seniors).
- **Fallback:** if Wi-Fi fails, switch to screenshots in `../28_MEDIA/`.
- **Close:** 10 rules + 1930 + "be a Guardian for your family."
