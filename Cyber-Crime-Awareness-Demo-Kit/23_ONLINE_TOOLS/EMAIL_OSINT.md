# Online Tools — Email / Breach


| Tool | Official URL | Purpose |
|---|---|---|
| Have I Been Pwned | https://haveibeenpwned.com | Check if an email appears in breaches |
| Mozilla Monitor | https://monitor.mozilla.org | Breach monitoring & alerts |
| EmailRep | https://emailrep.io | Reputation/profile of an email address |

**Input:** YOUR OWN email, or a reserved sample (`...@example.com`).
**Expected output:** breaches the email appears in and what data types leaked.
**Demo scenario:** Enter your own email; show breaches + "what leaked" (demo 03).
**Safety notes:** Only your own email or the fictional sample; get consent otherwise.
