# Electricity Bill Scam
**Tech Guardians · AWARENESS ONLY**

**REAL-WORLD SCENARIO**
SMS: "Your electricity will be disconnected tonight; your bill isn't updated.
Call this officer."

**HOW CRIMINAL APPROACHES VICTIM**
Threatening SMS/WhatsApp + a personal "officer" number.

**WHAT VICTIM SEES**
A disconnection warning and a helpful "officer" guiding a small payment/app install.

**LIVE DEMONSTRATION**
Show the sample message in `../24_SAMPLE_DATA/`; map to the UPI/APK tricks.

**WHAT CRIMINAL WANTS**
A "verification" payment, or installation of a malicious app for OTP theft.

**WHY VICTIM BELIEVES IT**
Fear of same-day disconnection + a confident "official".

**RED FLAGS**
Personal mobile number, same-day threat, asks to install an app or pay via link.

**WHAT VICTIM SHOULD DO**
Ignore; check bill in the official board app/website; call the official helpline.

**HOW TO PREVENT IT**
Know boards don't send such personal-number SMS; verify via official channels only.
