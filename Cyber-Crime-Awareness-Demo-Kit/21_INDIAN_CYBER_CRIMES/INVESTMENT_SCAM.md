# Investment / Trading Scam
**Tech Guardians · AWARENESS ONLY**

**REAL-WORLD SCENARIO**
A "mentor" group promises guaranteed high returns on stocks/crypto via a special app.

**HOW CRIMINAL APPROACHES VICTIM**
WhatsApp/Telegram "investment tips" groups, fake advisor, celebrity deepfakes.

**WHAT VICTIM SEES**
A slick app showing growing "profits" and testimonials.

**LIVE DEMONSTRATION**
Discuss the fake-app dashboard concept; link `FAKE_TRADING_APP.md`.

**WHAT CRIMINAL WANTS**
Ever-larger deposits; withdrawals blocked behind "tax/fees".

**WHY VICTIM BELIEVES IT**
Fake in-app profits + social proof + fear of missing out.

**RED FLAGS**
Guaranteed returns, off-store app, can't withdraw without paying "tax", pressure to add funds.

**WHAT VICTIM SHOULD DO**
Stop depositing; don't pay "withdrawal tax"; report to 1930 and SEBI channels.

**HOW TO PREVENT IT**
Use only SEBI-registered platforms; guaranteed returns = scam.
