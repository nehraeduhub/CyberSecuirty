# Card Skimming / ATM Fraud
**Tech Guardians · AWARENESS ONLY**

**REAL-WORLD SCENARIO**
A victim's card is cloned at an ATM or PoS and money is withdrawn or spent elsewhere.

**HOW CRIMINAL APPROACHES VICTIM**
A skimmer device over the card slot + a hidden camera or fake keypad captures card data and PIN.

**WHAT VICTIM SEES**
Nothing unusual at the time; later, unknown withdrawals/transactions appear.

**LIVE DEMONSTRATION**
Show what a skimmer/overlay looks like (images); explain magstripe vs chip/tap.

**WHAT CRIMINAL WANTS**
Card data + PIN to clone the card and withdraw/spend.

**WHY VICTIM BELIEVES IT**
The ATM/PoS looks normal; the overlay is subtle.

**RED FLAGS**
Loose/bulky card slot, sticky keypad, hidden camera, machines in isolated spots.

**WHAT VICTIM SHOULD DO**
Block the card immediately; report to bank + 1930; dispute transactions.

**HOW TO PREVENT IT**
Cover the keypad while entering PIN; use chip/tap; prefer bank-premises ATMs; enable transaction alerts & limits.
