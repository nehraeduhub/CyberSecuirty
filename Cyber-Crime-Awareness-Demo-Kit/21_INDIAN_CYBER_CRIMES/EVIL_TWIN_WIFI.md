# Evil Twin / Fake Public Wi-Fi
**Tech Guardians · AWARENESS ONLY**

**REAL-WORLD SCENARIO**
At a cafe/airport, a victim connects to free Wi-Fi named like the venue and their logins/traffic are captured.

**HOW CRIMINAL APPROACHES VICTIM**
The attacker runs a rogue hotspot with a familiar name; victims auto-join and send data through the attacker.

**WHAT VICTIM SEES**
A normal-looking free Wi-Fi list; no password or a fake captive portal asking to 'log in'.

**LIVE DEMONSTRATION**
Explain MITM on open Wi-Fi; link to the HTTP-vs-HTTPS network demo (19).

**WHAT CRIMINAL WANTS**
Credentials, session cookies, and anything sent over unencrypted connections.

**WHY VICTIM BELIEVES IT**
Free Wi-Fi is expected at such venues; the name matches.

**RED FLAGS**
Duplicate/odd network names, no WPA password, a captive portal asking for social/bank login.

**WHAT VICTIM SHOULD DO**
Disconnect; avoid sensitive logins; use a trusted VPN; forget the network.

**HOW TO PREVENT IT**
Use mobile data or a VPN on public Wi-Fi; verify the exact network name with staff; prefer HTTPS; turn off auto-join.
