# AI Voice Clone Scam
**Tech Guardians · AWARENESS ONLY**

**REAL-WORLD SCENARIO**
A parent gets a call in their child's exact voice: "Mom, I'm in trouble, send money now."

**HOW CRIMINAL APPROACHES VICTIM**
Clones a voice from public audio/social clips; calls with a crafted emergency.

**WHAT VICTIM SEES/HEARS**
A familiar voice, crying/panicked, demanding secrecy and speed.

**LIVE DEMONSTRATION**
Explain how little audio is needed to clone a voice; stress out-of-band verification.

**WHAT CRIMINAL WANTS**
Immediate money before the victim can verify.

**WHY VICTIM BELIEVES IT**
The voice is convincing and emotion overrides caution.

**RED FLAGS**
Urgent money, "don't tell anyone", refuses normal verification, slightly off phrasing.

**WHAT VICTIM SHOULD DO**
Hang up and call the person directly; ask a pre-agreed safe-word question; verify.

**HOW TO PREVENT IT**
Set a family safe word; limit public voice clips; agree "we always verify money calls".
