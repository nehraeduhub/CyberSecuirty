# OTP Scam
**Tech Guardians · AWARENESS ONLY**

**REAL-WORLD SCENARIO**
A caller needs "the OTP you just received" to process a refund/delivery/verification.

**HOW CRIMINAL APPROACHES VICTIM**
Call/SMS posing as bank/delivery/support, timing an OTP they triggered.

**WHAT VICTIM SEES**
A real OTP arriving on their phone exactly as the caller predicted.

**LIVE DEMONSTRATION**
Explain that the OTP authorises THEIR transaction, not a "verification".

**WHAT CRIMINAL WANTS**
The OTP, to complete a login/payment/registration they initiated.

**WHY VICTIM BELIEVES IT**
The OTP is genuine and arrives on cue, making the caller seem legitimate.

**RED FLAGS**
Anyone asking you to read out/forward an OTP; "share to verify/receive".

**WHAT VICTIM SHOULD DO**
Never share an OTP; read what the OTP SMS is actually authorising; hang up.

**HOW TO PREVENT IT**
Rule: OTP = YOUR approval. No legitimate staff ever needs it.
