# Phishing (Email/Web)
**Tech Guardians · AWARENESS ONLY**

**REAL-WORLD SCENARIO**
An email/site mimics a bank/employer and asks you to "verify" your login.

**HOW CRIMINAL APPROACHES VICTIM**
Spoofed email/SMS with a lookalike link to a fake login page.

**WHAT VICTIM SEES**
A familiar-looking login page urging immediate action.

**LIVE DEMONSTRATION**
`../08_PHISHING_AWARENESS/` local simulation + `../16_SUSPICIOUS_URL/`.

**WHAT CRIMINAL WANTS**
Your credentials (and then MFA codes / account access).

**WHY VICTIM BELIEVES IT**
Convincing branding, urgency, and a plausible pretext.

**RED FLAGS**
Login via link, lookalike domain, urgency, credential/OTP requests, spf/dkim fail.

**WHAT VICTIM SHOULD DO**
Don't log in from links; verify domain; enable MFA; if entered, reset + report.

**HOW TO PREVENT IT**
Use bookmarks/apps for logins; enable MFA; read the real domain.
