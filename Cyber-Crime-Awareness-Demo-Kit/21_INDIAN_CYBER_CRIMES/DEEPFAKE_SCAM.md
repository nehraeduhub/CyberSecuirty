# Deepfake Scam
**Tech Guardians · AWARENESS ONLY**

**REAL-WORLD SCENARIO**
A video shows a celebrity/official endorsing an investment, or a "relative" on
video asking for urgent money.

**HOW CRIMINAL APPROACHES VICTIM**
AI-generated video/face-swap shared via ads, WhatsApp, or live calls.

**WHAT VICTIM SEES**
A convincing video of a trusted face saying something they never said.

**LIVE DEMONSTRATION**
Show known deepfake tell-tales (lip-sync, blinking, lighting, edges) on sample clips.

**WHAT CRIMINAL WANTS**
Credibility for an investment/charity scam or an urgent money transfer.

**WHY VICTIM BELIEVES IT**
"Seeing is believing" — video feels like proof.

**RED FLAGS**
Unnatural lip-sync/blinking, odd lighting/edges, too-good endorsements, urgency.

**WHAT VICTIM SHOULD DO**
Verify via official channels; call the real person back; don't act on a video alone.

**HOW TO PREVENT IT**
Treat video as checkable, not proof; confirm big asks out-of-band.
