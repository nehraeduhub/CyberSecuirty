# Fake Trading App
**Tech Guardians · AWARENESS ONLY**

**REAL-WORLD SCENARIO**
A polished "trading/crypto" app shows soaring profits but blocks withdrawals.

**HOW CRIMINAL APPROACHES VICTIM**
Investment groups, fake advisors, and off-store app links.

**WHAT VICTIM SEES**
A professional dashboard with rising balances and "VIP" tips.

**LIVE DEMONSTRATION**
Discuss the fabricated dashboard; link `INVESTMENT_SCAM.md` and `../13_FAKE_APK/`.

**WHAT CRIMINAL WANTS**
Continuous deposits; withdrawals gated behind "tax/fees" that are also stolen.

**WHY VICTIM BELIEVES IT**
The in-app numbers look real; early small withdrawals may be allowed to build trust.

**RED FLAGS**
Off-store app, guaranteed/absurd returns, withdrawal "tax", pressure to deposit more.

**WHAT VICTIM SHOULD DO**
Stop depositing; don't pay "tax"; screenshot evidence; report to 1930 and SEBI.

**HOW TO PREVENT IT**
Use only SEBI-registered, store-listed platforms; verify before investing.
