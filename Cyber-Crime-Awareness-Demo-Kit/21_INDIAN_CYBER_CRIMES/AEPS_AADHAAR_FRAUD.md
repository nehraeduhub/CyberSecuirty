# AEPS / Aadhaar Biometric Fraud
**Tech Guardians · AWARENESS ONLY**

**REAL-WORLD SCENARIO**
Money vanishes from a victim's bank account via Aadhaar-enabled Payment System (AePS) — without any OTP or card.

**HOW CRIMINAL APPROACHES VICTIM**
Criminals use leaked Aadhaar numbers + cloned fingerprints (lifted from public documents/registry papers) on biometric PoS devices.

**WHAT VICTIM SEES**
An SMS that money was debited via AePS, though they shared no OTP or PIN.

**LIVE DEMONSTRATION**
Explain AePS: Aadhaar + fingerprint = withdrawal. Show how fingerprints/Aadhaar leak from public records.

**WHAT CRIMINAL WANTS**
Cash withdrawals from the victim's bank using cloned biometrics.

**WHY VICTIM BELIEVES IT**
They don't — it bypasses the victim entirely; victims notice only after money is gone.

**RED FLAGS**
Debits with 'AePS' in the SMS; no OTP involved; your Aadhaar/fingerprint shared on public forms.

**WHAT VICTIM SHOULD DO**
Lock Aadhaar biometrics on UIDAI; report to bank + 1930; file at cybercrime.gov.in.

**HOW TO PREVENT IT**
Lock/freeze Aadhaar biometrics (UIDAI), use masked Aadhaar, never share biometric/Aadhaar copies publicly.
