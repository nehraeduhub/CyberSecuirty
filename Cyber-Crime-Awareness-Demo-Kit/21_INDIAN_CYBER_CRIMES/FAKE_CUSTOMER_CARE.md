# Fake Customer Care
**Tech Guardians · AWARENESS ONLY**

**REAL-WORLD SCENARIO**
A user searches "bank/airline/wallet customer care" and calls a planted number.

**HOW CRIMINAL APPROACHES VICTIM**
Fake numbers seeded in search results, ads, and fake listings/maps.

**WHAT VICTIM SEES**
A "support agent" who sounds professional and promises to fix a refund/issue.

**LIVE DEMONSTRATION**
`../12_UPI_FRAUD/MOCK_PAYMENT_SCREENS/screens.md` — fake care flow.

**WHAT CRIMINAL WANTS**
Screen-share install, OTP/PIN, or a "small verification" payment.

**WHY VICTIM BELIEVES IT**
They initiated the call, so they trust the person who answers.

**RED FLAGS**
Number from search/ads, asks to install AnyDesk/TeamViewer, asks for OTP/PIN.

**WHAT VICTIM SHOULD DO**
Hang up; use the number on the official app/card back; never share OTP/screen.

**HOW TO PREVENT IT**
Save official numbers; never trust search-result support numbers.
