# SIM Swap
**Tech Guardians · AWARENESS ONLY**

**REAL-WORLD SCENARIO**
A victim's phone suddenly loses signal; soon their bank OTPs go to a criminal's SIM.

**HOW CRIMINAL APPROACHES VICTIM**
Social-engineers the telecom (using leaked personal data) to port/replace the SIM.

**WHAT VICTIM SEES**
"No service" on their phone, then unexpected transactions/OTPs they didn't get.

**LIVE DEMONSTRATION**
Explain the chain: data leak → SIM reissue → OTP interception.

**WHAT CRIMINAL WANTS**
Control of the victim's number to receive OTPs and reset accounts.

**WHY VICTIM BELIEVES IT**
They don't — it's an attack on the telecom; victims realise late.

**RED FLAGS**
Sudden prolonged "no service"; being asked to send a SIM/port SMS; unknown OTPs.

**WHAT VICTIM SHOULD DO**
Contact the telecom immediately to block/reissue; freeze bank; call 1930.

**HOW TO PREVENT IT**
Use app-based MFA (not just SMS); set a SIM/port lock/PIN; limit personal-data exposure.
