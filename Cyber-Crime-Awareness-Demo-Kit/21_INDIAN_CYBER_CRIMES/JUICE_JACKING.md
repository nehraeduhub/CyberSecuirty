# Juice Jacking (Public USB Charging)
**Tech Guardians · AWARENESS ONLY**

**REAL-WORLD SCENARIO**
A traveller charges their phone at a public USB port (airport/station/cafe) and later finds data stolen or malware installed.

**HOW CRIMINAL APPROACHES VICTIM**
A tampered public charging port or cable carries data lines, not just power, to pull files or push malware.

**WHAT VICTIM SEES**
Nothing obvious — just 'charging'; the phone may prompt 'Trust this computer?'.

**LIVE DEMONSTRATION**
Show the data vs power lines concept; demonstrate a USB 'data blocker'.

**WHAT CRIMINAL WANTS**
Access to phone files, or to install spyware during the charge.

**WHY VICTIM BELIEVES IT**
Charging seems harmless; the port looks official.

**RED FLAGS**
A charge prompt asking to 'trust'/'transfer files'; unknown cables left plugged in.

**WHAT VICTIM SHOULD DO**
Decline any data/trust prompt; disconnect; scan the device.

**HOW TO PREVENT IT**
Carry your own charger/power bank; use a USB 'data-blocker'; avoid public USB ports; charge from a wall socket.
