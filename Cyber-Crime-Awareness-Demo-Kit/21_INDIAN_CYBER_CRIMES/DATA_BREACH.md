# Data Breach
**Tech Guardians · AWARENESS ONLY**

**REAL-WORLD SCENARIO**
A service you used is breached; your email, phone, password and more leak online.

**HOW CRIMINAL APPROACHES VICTIM**
Uses leaked data for credential stuffing, targeted phishing, and SIM-swap prep.

**WHAT VICTIM SEES**
Breach-alert emails, spam, login attempts, or nothing — until an account is hit.

**LIVE DEMONSTRATION**
`../03_EMAIL_BREACH/` (Have I Been Pwned / Mozilla Monitor).

**WHAT CRIMINAL WANTS**
To reuse leaked credentials and personal data across your accounts.

**WHY VICTIM BELIEVES IT (follow-on scams)**
Attackers quote real leaked details to sound legitimate.

**RED FLAGS**
Unexpected login/OTP alerts; emails quoting an old password; spike in spam.

**WHAT VICTIM SHOULD DO**
Change reused passwords; enable MFA; monitor accounts; use breach-alert services.

**HOW TO PREVENT IT**
Unique passwords + manager + MFA; minimise data you share with services.
