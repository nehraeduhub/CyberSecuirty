# Vishing (Voice Phishing)
**Tech Guardians · AWARENESS ONLY**

**REAL-WORLD SCENARIO**
A caller claims to be your bank's fraud team and needs details to "secure" your account.

**HOW CRIMINAL APPROACHES VICTIM**
Spoofed caller ID, scripted authority, manufactured urgency.

**WHAT VICTIM SEES/HEARS**
A professional-sounding agent referencing some real detail about them.

**LIVE DEMONSTRATION**
Role-play the call; link `../05_PHONE_NUMBER/` (caller-ID spoofing).

**WHAT CRIMINAL WANTS**
Card number/CVV/OTP/PIN or remote access.

**WHY VICTIM BELIEVES IT**
Caller ID + a known detail + urgent tone.

**RED FLAGS**
Asks for OTP/PIN/CVV, pressure, "don't hang up", remote-app install.

**WHAT VICTIM SHOULD DO**
Hang up; call back on the official number; never share secrets/screen.

**HOW TO PREVENT IT**
Banks never ask for OTP/PIN/CVV; verify by calling official numbers.
