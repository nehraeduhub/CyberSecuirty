# QR Scam
**Tech Guardians · AWARENESS ONLY**

**REAL-WORLD SCENARIO**
"Scan this QR to receive your refund/prize/payment."

**HOW CRIMINAL APPROACHES VICTIM**
Chat, email, poster, or a replaced QR sticker on a shop/parking meter.

**WHAT VICTIM SEES**
A QR that claims to credit money, or a parking/payment QR that looks official.

**LIVE DEMONSTRATION**
`../09_QR_CODE_SCAM/` — "receive vs authorise payment".

**WHAT CRIMINAL WANTS**
To make scanning trigger a PAY request the victim approves with a PIN, or open a
phishing link.

**WHY VICTIM BELIEVES IT**
Belief that scanning is harmless/that a QR can "send" you money.

**RED FLAGS**
Any QR to "receive" money; PIN prompt after scanning; QR from strangers/stickers.

**WHAT VICTIM SHOULD DO**
Don't scan to receive; read the debit/credit text; verify independently.

**HOW TO PREVENT IT**
Never enter a PIN to receive; be wary of pasted/printed QR codes.
