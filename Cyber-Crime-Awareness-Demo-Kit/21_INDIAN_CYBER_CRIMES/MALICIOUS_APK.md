# Malicious APK
**Tech Guardians · AWARENESS ONLY**

**REAL-WORLD SCENARIO**
A WhatsApp forward offers a "subsidy/wedding invite/banking" app as a direct APK.

**HOW CRIMINAL APPROACHES VICTIM**
Off-store APK via link/chat with urgent or enticing framing.

**WHAT VICTIM SEES**
An app that asks for SMS, Accessibility and overlay permissions.

**LIVE DEMONSTRATION**
Read-only analysis workflow in `../13_FAKE_APK/` (VirusTotal/MobSF/JADX/Apktool).

**WHAT CRIMINAL WANTS**
To read OTP SMS, overlay fake screens, and drain accounts/spread to contacts.

**WHY VICTIM BELIEVES IT**
Enticing offer + "official" look + trust in the forwarder.

**RED FLAGS**
Off-store install, SMS+Accessibility+overlay permissions, urgency.

**WHAT VICTIM SHOULD DO**
Don't install; if installed, disconnect net, uninstall/factory-reset, change banking creds, report.

**HOW TO PREVENT IT**
Install only from official stores; scrutinise permissions; keep Play Protect on.
