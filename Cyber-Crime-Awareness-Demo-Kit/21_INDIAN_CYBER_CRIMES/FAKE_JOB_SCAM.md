# Fake Job / Task Scam
**Tech Guardians · AWARENESS ONLY**

**REAL-WORLD SCENARIO**
"Work from home! Like videos / rate hotels and earn daily. Pay a small deposit to
start / to withdraw."

**HOW CRIMINAL APPROACHES VICTIM**
WhatsApp/Telegram messages, fake HR, "task-based earning" groups.

**WHAT VICTIM SEES**
Small early payouts that build trust, then larger "deposit to unlock earnings".

**LIVE DEMONSTRATION**
Show the chat sample in `../24_SAMPLE_DATA/`; explain the deposit trap.

**WHAT CRIMINAL WANTS**
Escalating "deposits"/"taxes"/"unlocking fees" that are never returned.

**WHY VICTIM BELIEVES IT**
Real small payouts early + social proof in the group + easy-money appeal.

**RED FLAGS**
Pay-to-earn, deposits to withdraw, Telegram "tasks", unrealistic daily income.

**WHAT VICTIM SHOULD DO**
Stop paying; never deposit to "unlock" earnings; report; warn others.

**HOW TO PREVENT IT**
No real job asks you to pay to work or to withdraw your own earnings.
