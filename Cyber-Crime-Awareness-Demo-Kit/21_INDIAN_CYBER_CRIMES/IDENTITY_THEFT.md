# Identity Theft
**Tech Guardians · AWARENESS ONLY**

**REAL-WORLD SCENARIO**
Someone uses the victim's Aadhaar/PAN/photos to open accounts, take loans, or
commit fraud in their name.

**HOW CRIMINAL APPROACHES VICTIM**
Harvests leaked/overshared documents and personal data.

**WHAT VICTIM SEES**
Unknown loans/accounts, credit issues, or legal notices.

**LIVE DEMONSTRATION**
Tie to `../02_PEOPLE_EXPOSURE/`, `../03_EMAIL_BREACH/`, document-metadata (demo 20).

**WHAT CRIMINAL WANTS**
To impersonate the victim for financial gain.

**WHY VICTIM BELIEVES IT (third parties)**
Genuine documents make the impersonation convincing to banks/services.

**RED FLAGS**
Unrecognised accounts/loans, credit-report surprises, misused documents.

**WHAT VICTIM SHOULD DO**
Report to police/1930; dispute with banks/bureaus; lock Aadhaar biometrics; keep records.

**HOW TO PREVENT IT**
Mask/limit sharing of ID documents; use masked Aadhaar; monitor credit; shred/strip metadata.
