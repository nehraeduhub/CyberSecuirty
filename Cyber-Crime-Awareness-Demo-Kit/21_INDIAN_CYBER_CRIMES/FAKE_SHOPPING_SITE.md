# Fake Shopping Website / Festive Sale Scam
**Tech Guardians · AWARENESS ONLY**

**REAL-WORLD SCENARIO**
A victim orders from a too-good-to-be-true online 'sale' and receives nothing — or a fake product.

**HOW CRIMINAL APPROACHES VICTIM**
Ads on social media lead to lookalike/fake stores with huge discounts and UPI/card checkout.

**WHAT VICTIM SEES**
A slick store, big discounts, countdown timers, fake reviews, UPI/card payment.

**LIVE DEMONSTRATION**
Analyse the URL (demo 16); check domain age/reviews; link to `SUSPICIOUS_URL` flow.

**WHAT CRIMINAL WANTS**
Payment with no delivery, or stolen card details.

**WHY VICTIM BELIEVES IT**
Festive urgency + unbelievable prices + professional-looking site.

**RED FLAGS**
Brand-new/odd domain, only prepaid, no real contact, prices far below market.

**WHAT VICTIM SHOULD DO**
Dispute the payment with your bank; report; avoid the site. 

**HOW TO PREVENT IT**
Buy from known stores; prefer COD/verified sellers; check domain age, reviews and return policy.
