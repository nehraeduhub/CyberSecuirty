# KYC Update Scam
**Tech Guardians · AWARENESS ONLY**

**REAL-WORLD SCENARIO**
"Your bank/wallet KYC expires today; update now or account blocked."

**HOW CRIMINAL APPROACHES VICTIM**
SMS/WhatsApp with a link or a number, or a fake "KYC app".

**WHAT VICTIM SEES**
An urgent KYC deadline and a form/app requesting full details + OTP.

**LIVE DEMONSTRATION**
Phishing page (`../08_PHISHING_AWARENESS/`) + fake KYC APK story (`../13_FAKE_APK/`).

**WHAT CRIMINAL WANTS**
Full card/bank details, OTPs, and credentials.

**WHY VICTIM BELIEVES IT**
KYC is a familiar, real requirement; the deadline creates panic.

**RED FLAGS**
KYC "today or blocked", links/apps for KYC, requests for OTP/full card number.

**WHAT VICTIM SHOULD DO**
Do KYC only in-branch or via the official app; never via links; call the bank.

**HOW TO PREVENT IT**
Know banks don't do urgent KYC over links/OTP; verify with your branch.
