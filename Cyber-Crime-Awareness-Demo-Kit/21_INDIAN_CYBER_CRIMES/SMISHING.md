# Smishing (SMS Phishing)
**Tech Guardians · AWARENESS ONLY**

**REAL-WORLD SCENARIO**
An SMS: "Your account is blocked / parcel held / KYC due — click here."

**HOW CRIMINAL APPROACHES VICTIM**
Bulk SMS with shortened/lookalike links, often spoofed sender IDs.

**WHAT VICTIM SEES**
A short, urgent text with a tappable link.

**LIVE DEMONSTRATION**
Dissect sample SMS in `../24_SAMPLE_DATA/`; scan links per `../16_SUSPICIOUS_URL/`.

**WHAT CRIMINAL WANTS**
To drive a tap to a phishing page or a malicious APK.

**WHY VICTIM BELIEVES IT**
Phones feel personal; short texts bypass scrutiny.

**RED FLAGS**
Links in SMS, shorteners, urgency, misspellings, unknown senders.

**WHAT VICTIM SHOULD DO**
Don't tap; verify via official app; report spam; delete.

**HOW TO PREVENT IT**
Never act on SMS links; use official apps; enable spam filtering.
