# Ransomware (Awareness)
**Tech Guardians · AWARENESS ONLY**

**REAL-WORLD SCENARIO**
An organisation or individual finds files encrypted with a ransom note demanding crypto payment.

**HOW CRIMINAL APPROACHES VICTIM**
Entry via phishing attachment, malicious download, or exposed/weak remote access (RDP).

**WHAT VICTIM SEES**
A ransom note, encrypted/locked files, a countdown and a crypto wallet demand.

**LIVE DEMONSTRATION**
Explain the chain (phish → execute → encrypt) conceptually; link to `PHISHING.md` — no live malware.

**WHAT CRIMINAL WANTS**
Payment in cryptocurrency; often data is also stolen for double extortion.

**WHY VICTIM BELIEVES IT**
Fear of losing data/operations + a deadline.

**RED FLAGS**
Unexpected attachments, files renamed/locked, slow systems, a ransom message.

**WHAT VICTIM SHOULD DO**
Disconnect from the network; don't pay (no guarantee); restore from backups; report to CERT-In/1930.

**HOW TO PREVENT IT**
Keep offline backups; patch systems; MFA on remote access; staff phishing awareness; least privilege.
