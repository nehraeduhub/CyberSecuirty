# Fake Charity / Disaster Donation Scam
**Tech Guardians · AWARENESS ONLY**

**REAL-WORLD SCENARIO**
After a disaster, a victim donates to a 'relief fund' that is actually a scammer's account.

**HOW CRIMINAL APPROACHES VICTIM**
Emotional appeals on social media/WhatsApp with fake NGO names, QR codes and UPI IDs.

**WHAT VICTIM SEES**
A heart-wrenching post/image, an urgent appeal, and a QR/UPI to donate 'now'.

**LIVE DEMONSTRATION**
Verify the QR/UPI (demo 09); check the NGO's official registration; link to `QR_SCAM.md`.

**WHAT CRIMINAL WANTS**
Donations diverted to the scammer; sometimes personal/card data too.

**WHY VICTIM BELIEVES IT**
Compassion + urgency + a cause that feels real.

**RED FLAGS**
Unverified NGO, pressure to give immediately, personal UPI/QR, no official receipt/80G details.

**WHAT VICTIM SHOULD DO**
Stop; verify the charity officially; donate via the NGO's official website only; report if scammed.

**HOW TO PREVENT IT**
Give only to verified, registered charities via their official channels; be wary of forwarded appeals and QR codes.
