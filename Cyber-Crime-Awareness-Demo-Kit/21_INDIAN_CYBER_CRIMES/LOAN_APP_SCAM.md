# Instant Loan App Scam
**Tech Guardians · AWARENESS ONLY**

**REAL-WORLD SCENARIO**
An instant-loan app gives a small loan, then harasses and blackmails using the
borrower's contacts and photos.

**HOW CRIMINAL APPROACHES VICTIM**
Off-store loan APKs that demand contacts, gallery and SMS access.

**WHAT VICTIM SEES**
Fast approval, then abusive recovery calls and morphed photos sent to contacts.

**LIVE DEMONSTRATION**
Permission checklist in `../13_FAKE_APK/PERMISSION_CHECKLIST.md`.

**WHAT CRIMINAL WANTS**
Extortion via harvested contacts/photos and inflated "fees".

**WHY VICTIM BELIEVES IT**
Urgent cash need + easy approval.

**RED FLAGS**
App wants contacts/gallery/SMS, off-store, tiny tenure, huge hidden charges, threats.

**WHAT VICTIM SHOULD DO**
Stop paying extortion; revoke permissions; keep evidence; report to 1930/police.

**HOW TO PREVENT IT**
Borrow only from RBI-regulated lenders; deny contacts/gallery to loan apps.
