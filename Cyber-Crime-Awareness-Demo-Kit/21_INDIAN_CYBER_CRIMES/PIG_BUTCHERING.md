# Pig Butchering (Romance + Crypto Investment)
**Tech Guardians · AWARENESS ONLY**

**REAL-WORLD SCENARIO**
A friendly stranger builds a weeks-long relationship, then introduces a 'guaranteed' crypto/trading app where profits soar — until withdrawals are blocked.

**HOW CRIMINAL APPROACHES VICTIM**
Long grooming on dating/social apps, slowly moving to a fake investment platform.

**WHAT VICTIM SEES**
A caring 'partner/mentor', a slick app with rising balances, small test withdrawals that work.

**LIVE DEMONSTRATION**
Tie to `INVESTMENT_SCAM.md` and `FAKE_TRADING_APP.md`; show the fabricated dashboard concept.

**WHAT CRIMINAL WANTS**
Ever-larger deposits; withdrawals gated behind 'tax/fees' that are also stolen.

**WHY VICTIM BELIEVES IT**
Emotional trust built over time + fake proof of profits + fear of missing out.

**RED FLAGS**
Fast online intimacy, steering to an off-store app, guaranteed returns, can't withdraw without paying.

**WHAT VICTIM SHOULD DO**
Stop depositing; don't pay 'withdrawal tax'; preserve chats; report to 1930 & SEBI.

**HOW TO PREVENT IT**
Never mix dating with investing; use only SEBI-registered platforms; guaranteed returns = scam.
