# Matrimonial / Dating Fraud
**Tech Guardians · AWARENESS ONLY**

**REAL-WORLD SCENARIO**
A victim on a matrimonial/dating site is courted by a 'well-settled' match who soon needs money for an emergency.

**HOW CRIMINAL APPROACHES VICTIM**
Fake polished profiles (often claiming to be abroad/NRI/army) build trust, then invent a crisis.

**WHAT VICTIM SEES**
An attractive profile, loving messages, then a customs/hospital/visa 'emergency' needing urgent funds.

**LIVE DEMONSTRATION**
Tie to `PIG_BUTCHERING.md`; discuss profile verification and reverse image search (demo 06).

**WHAT CRIMINAL WANTS**
Money transfers, gift cards, or personal/ID data for identity theft.

**WHY VICTIM BELIEVES IT**
Emotional investment + a convincing back-story + urgency.

**RED FLAGS**
Refuses video calls, quick declarations, money emergencies, 'stuck at customs/airport'.

**WHAT VICTIM SHOULD DO**
Stop sending money; reverse-search photos; verify identity; report to the platform & 1930.

**HOW TO PREVENT IT**
Never send money to someone you haven't met; verify via video; keep financial and romantic lives separate.
