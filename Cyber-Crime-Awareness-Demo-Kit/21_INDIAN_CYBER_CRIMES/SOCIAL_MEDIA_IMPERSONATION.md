# Social Media Impersonation
**Tech Guardians · AWARENESS ONLY**

**REAL-WORLD SCENARIO**
A cloned profile of you (same photo/name) messages your friends asking for money
or sharing scam links.

**HOW CRIMINAL APPROACHES VICTIM**
Copies public photos/details to create a lookalike account.

**WHAT VICTIM SEES**
Friends asking "did you message me?"; a duplicate profile.

**LIVE DEMONSTRATION**
Link to `../02_PEOPLE_EXPOSURE/` and `../06_PHOTO_PRIVACY/` (reuse of photos).

**WHAT CRIMINAL WANTS**
To defraud the victim's friends or damage reputation.

**WHY VICTIM BELIEVES IT (friends)**
The profile looks identical and messages feel personal.

**RED FLAGS**
Duplicate account, friend requests from "you", money/link requests.

**WHAT VICTIM SHOULD DO**
Report/clone-takedown; warn contacts; lock down privacy; keep evidence; report.

**HOW TO PREVENT IT**
Limit public photos/details; verify accounts; enable privacy + MFA.
