# Lottery / KBC Lucky Draw Scam
**Tech Guardians · AWARENESS ONLY**

**REAL-WORLD SCENARIO**
A message/call says the victim won a huge 'KBC lottery' and must pay a processing fee to claim it.

**HOW CRIMINAL APPROACHES VICTIM**
WhatsApp/SMS/call with fake prize letters, official-looking logos and an 'RBI/officer' contact.

**WHAT VICTIM SEES**
A congratulations message, a prize amount, and a small 'GST/processing fee' to release winnings.

**LIVE DEMONSTRATION**
Show a fake prize letter prop (clearly watermarked); link to `OTP_SCAM.md`.

**WHAT CRIMINAL WANTS**
Repeated 'fees/taxes' to release a prize that never exists.

**WHY VICTIM BELIEVES IT**
Greed/hope + official-looking documents + authority names.

**RED FLAGS**
You never entered any lottery; fees to receive a prize; pressure and secrecy.

**WHAT VICTIM SHOULD DO**
Don't pay; block; report. No legitimate prize needs an upfront fee.

**HOW TO PREVENT IT**
Ignore unsolicited winnings; never pay to 'receive' money; verify with official sources.
