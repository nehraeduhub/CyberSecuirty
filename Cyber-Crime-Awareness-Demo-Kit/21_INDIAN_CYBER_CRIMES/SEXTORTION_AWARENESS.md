# Sextortion Awareness
**Tech Guardians · AWARENESS ONLY (sensitive topic — handle with care)**

**REAL-WORLD SCENARIO**
A stranger befriends the victim, moves to a video call, records compromising
footage (real or morphed), then demands money to not share it.

**HOW CRIMINAL APPROACHES VICTIM**
Fake attractive profile → quick intimacy → video call → recording → blackmail.

**WHAT VICTIM SEES**
A threatening message with a clip/screenshot and a payment demand/deadline.

**LIVE DEMONSTRATION**
Discuss the pattern only — never show explicit content. Emphasise support, not shame.

**WHAT CRIMINAL WANTS**
Repeated payments; paying never ends it.

**WHY VICTIM BELIEVES IT**
Fear, shame, and the threat of exposure to family/contacts.

**RED FLAGS**
Fast online intimacy, push to video, requests to "go private", sudden threats.

**WHAT VICTIM SHOULD DO**
Do NOT pay; stop contact; don't delete evidence; tell a trusted person; report to
1930 / cybercrime.gov.in (women/children: dedicated portals exist). It's not your fault.

**HOW TO PREVENT IT**
Be cautious with strangers/video calls; limit who can contact/see you; talk to youth openly.
