# Digital Arrest
**Tech Guardians · AWARENESS ONLY**

**REAL-WORLD SCENARIO**
A retiree gets a call claiming a parcel in their name holds illegal items; the
call escalates to "police" and "CBI" on video, declaring a "digital arrest".

**HOW CRIMINAL APPROACHES VICTIM**
Spoofed call → "courier/customs" → transfer to fake "police/CBI" → video call with
uniform and fake warrant.

**WHAT VICTIM SEES**
An officer-looking person, a "police" backdrop, a fake arrest warrant/RBI notice
with their name.

**LIVE DEMONSTRATION**
Run the role-play in `../11_DIGITAL_ARREST/ROLEPLAY_SCRIPT.md` and the fake notice prop.

**WHAT CRIMINAL WANTS**
To keep the victim isolated on camera and have them transfer "verification" money.

**WHY VICTIM BELIEVES IT**
Authority (police/CBI) + fear of jail + realistic props + "tell no one" isolation.

**RED FLAGS**
"Digital arrest" (not real), police on video call, "transfer to verify", secrecy,
threats, extreme urgency.

**WHAT VICTIM SHOULD DO**
Disconnect, tell family, don't transfer, call 112 / 1930, report at cybercrime.gov.in.

**HOW TO PREVENT IT**
Know no agency arrests via video or asks for money to "verify"; brief seniors; stay calm.
