# UPI Fraud
**Tech Guardians · AWARENESS ONLY**

**REAL-WORLD SCENARIO**
A seller online is told to "scan to receive" payment, or a buyer "accidentally"
sends extra and asks for a refund.

**HOW CRIMINAL APPROACHES VICTIM**
Marketplace chat, fake refund, collect request, or a fake "customer care" number.

**WHAT VICTIM SEES**
A payment request/QR labelled "receive", or a support agent guiding them to pay.

**LIVE DEMONSTRATION**
Use `../12_UPI_FRAUD/` mock screens and the "PIN = PAY" rule.

**WHAT CRIMINAL WANTS**
To make the victim authorise a debit (enter UPI PIN) or reveal OTP.

**WHY VICTIM BELIEVES IT**
Confusion about receive vs pay + urgency + a "helpful" agent.

**RED FLAGS**
Entering a PIN to "receive", a collect request you didn't start, support numbers
from search, requests to install screen-share apps.

**WHAT VICTIM SHOULD DO**
Cancel, never enter PIN to receive, verify in own bank app, call bank + 1930.

**HOW TO PREVENT IT**
Remember PIN = PAY; share only your UPI ID to receive; set UPI limits.
