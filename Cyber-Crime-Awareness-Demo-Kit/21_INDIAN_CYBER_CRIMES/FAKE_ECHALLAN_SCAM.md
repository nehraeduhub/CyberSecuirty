# Fake e-Challan / Traffic Fine Scam
**Tech Guardians · AWARENESS ONLY**

**REAL-WORLD SCENARIO**
An SMS claims a pending traffic e-challan with a link to 'pay now or licence suspended'.

**HOW CRIMINAL APPROACHES VICTIM**
Smishing with a spoofed 'VAHAN/traffic police' sender ID and a lookalike payment link or malicious APK.

**WHAT VICTIM SEES**
A realistic challan message, vehicle-sounding details, urgency, and a 'pay fine' button.

**LIVE DEMONSTRATION**
Dissect the SMS; show the real domain is not a gov site; link to `PHISHING.md`/`SMISHING.md`.

**WHAT CRIMINAL WANTS**
Card/UPI details via a fake payment page, or an APK that steals OTPs.

**WHY VICTIM BELIEVES IT**
Many people do get challans; the timing and official tone feel plausible.

**RED FLAGS**
Links in SMS, non-gov domain, urgency, 'install app to pay', asks for card/OTP.

**WHAT VICTIM SHOULD DO**
Check challans only on the official echallan.parivahan.gov.in / state portal; never pay via SMS links.

**HOW TO PREVENT IT**
Pay fines only through official government portals; treat all challan SMS links as suspicious.
