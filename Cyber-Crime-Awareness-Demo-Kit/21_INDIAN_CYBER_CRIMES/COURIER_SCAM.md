# Courier / Parcel Scam
**Tech Guardians · AWARENESS ONLY**

**REAL-WORLD SCENARIO**
"Your parcel is held at customs; pay a small fee," or "illegal items found in your
parcel" (gateway to digital arrest).

**HOW CRIMINAL APPROACHES VICTIM**
Call/SMS posing as FedEx/DHL/India Post/customs.

**WHAT VICTIM SEES**
A tracking "issue", a small redelivery/customs fee, or an alarming legal claim.

**LIVE DEMONSTRATION**
Link to `../13_FAKE_APK/` (tracking APK) and `../11_DIGITAL_ARREST/`.

**WHAT CRIMINAL WANTS**
A "fee" payment with OTP theft, or to escalate into the digital-arrest scam.

**WHY VICTIM BELIEVES IT**
Many people are awaiting a real delivery; the timing feels plausible.

**RED FLAGS**
Unexpected fees via link, requests for OTP, escalation to "police/customs case".

**WHAT VICTIM SHOULD DO**
Track only via the official courier app/site; never pay via a link; hang up on
"customs case" calls and report.

**HOW TO PREVENT IT**
Use official tracking; couriers don't collect customs fees via random links.
