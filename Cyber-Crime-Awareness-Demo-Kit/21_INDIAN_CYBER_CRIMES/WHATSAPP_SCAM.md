# WhatsApp Scam / Takeover
**Tech Guardians · AWARENESS ONLY**

**REAL-WORLD SCENARIO**
A "friend" asks you to forward a 6-digit code, then your account is taken over and
your contacts get money requests.

**HOW CRIMINAL APPROACHES VICTIM**
"Wrong number / security code" trick; later, money requests to your contacts.

**WHAT VICTIM SEES**
A plausible request for a code, then messages from "you" asking contacts for money.

**LIVE DEMONSTRATION**
`../10_WHATSAPP_SCAM/` mock screens + two-step verification setup.

**WHAT CRIMINAL WANTS**
Your account (the 6-digit code) to defraud your contacts.

**WHY VICTIM BELIEVES IT**
Appears to come from a friend; helping feels natural.

**RED FLAGS**
Anyone asking for your code/OTP; a contact requesting money and refusing a call.

**WHAT VICTIM SHOULD DO**
Never share the code; enable two-step PIN; remove unknown linked devices; warn contacts.

**HOW TO PREVENT IT**
Two-step verification ON; "call me first" family rule for money requests.
