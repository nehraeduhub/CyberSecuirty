SIMULATED PHISHING PAGE

🛑 This was a simulated phishing page.

Nothing you typed was sent or saved. There is no server, no storage, and no network request behind this page. In a real attack, the moment you pressed "Sign in", your username and password would be in a criminal's hands.

🎣 How the attacker would try to trick you

  • Lookalike URL: nimbupay-secure-login.verify-account.example — the real brand name is only a subdomain word; the actual domain is verify-account.example, which the attacker owns.
  • No padlock / HTTP: the address starts with http:// (🔓) — no encryption.
  • Urgency & fear: a countdown and "account suspended" to make you rush.
  • Authority & branding: a familiar-looking logo and colours to feel official.
  • Credential capture: it asks for your password directly on a page you reached from a link.

🛡️ How to protect yourself

  • Read the domain right-to-left: find the real domain (the part just before the first single /). Here that's verify-account.example, not NimbuPay.
  • Never log in from a link in SMS/email/WhatsApp. Open the official app or type the known website address yourself.
  • Check for HTTPS and the correct spelling of the domain — but remember, HTTPS alone does not mean "safe".
  • Slow down. Urgency is the scam. Real services let you verify at your own pace.
  • Turn on MFA. Even a stolen password is far less useful with a second factor.
  • When unsure, call the official number on the back of your card / official site.
